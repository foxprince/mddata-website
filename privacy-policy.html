Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2024

We are committed to protecting the privacy of clients, prospects, and visitors who interact with MDdata. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the safeguards we apply to keep it secure. The structure and scope are inspired by industry-standard disclosures such as Infoway's publicly available notice.

Information We Collect

We collect information when you browse our sites, subscribe to MDdata services, participate in product evaluations, or communicate with our teams. The data falls into several categories:

  • Account Identifiers: name, company, email, phone number, billing profile, and authentication credentials.
  • Usage Analytics: IP address, device metadata, pages viewed, session durations, clickstream details, API request diagnostics, and crash logs.
  • Regulatory Records: KYC/KYB documentation, contract signatures, compliance questionnaires, and audit trails mandated by exchanges or regulators.
  • Communications: support requests, sales notes, meeting recordings, and survey responses that help us tailor services to your needs.

How We Use Information

We process personal and system data only for legitimate business purposes aligned with the delivery of capital markets infrastructure:

  • Enabling user authentication, authorization, and entitlements across MDdata web, API, and streaming services.
  • Providing onboarding, billing, incident response, and customer support.
  • Monitoring platform performance, detecting abuse, and enhancing the resilience of low-latency data pipelines.
  • Complying with contractual obligations and statutory requirements, including financial crime prevention and tax reporting.
  • Delivering product updates, research newsletters, or event invitations (with opt-out options in every message).

Legal Basis

Processing activities follow applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR, PIPL, and CCPA). Depending on your relationship with MDdata, the legal basis may be contractual necessity, legitimate business interest, legal obligation, or explicit consent. We perform balancing tests to ensure legitimate interests do not override your fundamental rights.

How We Share Information

We share information strictly on a need-to-know basis:

  • Vendors & Sub-processors: cloud hosting, security monitoring, payment processing, customer success tools.
  • Exchange & Regulatory Counterparties: when routing requests, fulfilling data redistribution audits, or responding to lawful orders.
  • Corporate Events: prospective investors or acquirers may access anonymized or aggregated data under NDA.

We never sell personal data. Every third party must sign data protection agreements and implement security safeguards equivalent to MDdata’s standards.

International Transfers

Our infrastructure is hosted in multiple regions. When data crosses borders, we implement standard contractual clauses, regional access controls, and encryption in transit and at rest. Users can request region-specific processing if mandated by exchange participation agreements.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to deliver services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. Trading-related telemetry may be retained for seven years to satisfy audit requirements, while marketing consents are scrubbed within 30 days after unsubscribe.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may be entitled to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you.
  • Object to or restrict processing, including profiling for product analytics.
  • Export your data in portable format.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

Submit requests via privacy@mddata.cc. We verify identity before acting on sensitive requests.

Security Measures

We employ network isolation, hardware-backed key management, continuous vulnerability scanning, and 24/7 SOC monitoring. Employees undergo security training, and access is governed by the principle of least privilege.

Policy Updates

We may amend this Privacy Policy to reflect regulatory changes or product enhancements. Material updates will be communicated via email and dashboard notifications at least 15 days prior to enforcement.
